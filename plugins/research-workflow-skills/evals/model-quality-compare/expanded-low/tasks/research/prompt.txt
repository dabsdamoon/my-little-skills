'In what regions does increased education spending positively impact per capita GDP?'
'What is the impact of increased education expenditure on per capita GDP in developing countries?'

    "groups":{"COUNTRY_CODE":{"label":"Country Group",
       "level":{"n":INTEGER,"pearson_r":NUMBER_OR_NULL,"ols_slope":NUMBER_OR_NULL},
       "difference":{"n":INTEGER,"pearson_r":NUMBER_OR_NULL,"ols_slope":NUMBER_OR_NULL}}}}
   Include each Country Code with the two specified series. Education is X; income

3. report.md explaining the original questions, what variables/data were actually

No network, installs, other models, outside directories or hidden graders. Complete
the analysis files, not just a proposal. This is a small observational-data analysis,
not a demand for an expensive training run or a definitive causal claim.
