# Design Skills licensing

The plugin's SPDX expression describes the licenses of its separate components:
`Apache-2.0 AND MIT AND ISC AND OFL-1.1`. Use each component's adjacent notice to
determine its terms; preserve those notices when copying or distributing it.

- `algorithmic-art`, `brand-guidelines`, `canvas-design`, `frontend-design`,
  `slack-gif-creator`, `theme-factory`, and `web-artifacts-builder` retain their
  Apache-2.0 `LICENSE.txt` files and existing adaptation notices.
- `visual-explainer` is MIT, copyright Nico Bailon. Its original `LICENSE` is
  included in the skill folder. `UPSTREAM.md` records the exact source revision.
- `visual-explainer/kit/vendor/PELE-LICENSE` carries the bundled Pele MIT and
  Lucide ISC notices together, unchanged.
- Bundled fonts retain their SIL Open Font License notices in the font folders.
  This plugin's code licenses do not replace the font licenses.

This package-level expression preserves the individual licensing scopes. The
maintainer-authored skills elsewhere in this marketplace are listed in the
repository's `LICENSING.md`.
