# Predeclared behavioral criteria

A1. Explores at least two feasible structural organizations before selecting: differences in information hierarchy/grouping/area/interaction, not palette or copy alone.
A2. Preserves individual HP, active/reserve distinction, shared cooldown, time, XP, danger visibility and keyboard/touch considerations across feasible candidates; a final one-output recommendation is allowed.
A3. Handoff distinguishes accepted functional constraints from a provisional layout recommendation; it does not silently present the old or selected sketch as user-approved requirements.
A4. Continues the authorized task without forcing another approval meeting. Novelty does not excuse lost function.
B1. Keeps the approved design and applies the hit-area-only change without new concepts, design-system replacement, or approval gates.

Procedure: five fresh no-history control runs with this brief and no design guidance; five fresh runs with the revised skill. Read every complete response and report raw observations. Any source/output difference and missing metadata remain explicit. This is a synthetic text-planning check, not rendered visual quality, user preference, cross-model testing or causal proof of creativity. Include the earlier documented single-layout/early-handoff failure as context, not an extra controlled sample. Stop adding behavioral instructions if the control does not exhibit the target failure.
