Modified by the my-little-skills maintainers on 2026-11-04. This is a local adaptation, not an unchanged upstream release. The entrypoint condenses subject-based design, intentional typography and motion, functional copy, and rendered self-critique. It keeps upstream's calibration list of generated-page defaults, its typographic tells, the two-pass plan review with the similar-prompt check, note-keeping across passes, the line-length guidance, and the CSS specificity warning. Local guidance adds scope routing, conditional structural comparisons, novelty-aware final selection with bounded reconsideration, explicit requirement/recommendation separation, proportionate handoffs, and a review of state visibility that derives feedback and functional motion from the user's flow. Reference transfer, causal motion, and temporal verification are developed further in the 2026-11-04 update. It does not prescribe one visual style or establish an empirically proven increase in creativity or usability.

Additional source reviewed: Benji Taylor's [Stageshow, commit 802057409787133703c56001080169e347305572](https://github.com/benjitaylor/skills/tree/802057409787133703c56001080169e347305572/stageshow), especially its [entrypoint](https://github.com/benjitaylor/skills/blob/802057409787133703c56001080169e347305572/stageshow/SKILL.md) and references on [choreography](https://github.com/benjitaylor/skills/blob/802057409787133703c56001080169e347305572/stageshow/references/choreography.md), [motion](https://github.com/benjitaylor/skills/blob/802057409787133703c56001080169e347305572/stageshow/references/motion.md), and [rehearsal](https://github.com/benjitaylor/skills/blob/802057409787133703c56001080169e347305572/stageshow/references/rehearse.md).
